FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

How long does AC service take?

Most standard AC services can be completed within a reasonable service appointment, usually taking around 45–60 minutes, depending on the type and condition of the unit. Our trained technicians carefully inspect the AC, clean the filters and essential components,check cooling performance, and identify any potential issues. For deep cleaning, repairs, gas refilling, or more complex problems, the service may take additional time to ensure the AC is properly inspected and working efficiently.

Do you provide doorstep service?

Yes, we provide convenient doorstep AC service at your preferred location. Our trained technicians visit your home or office with the necessary tools and equipment to inspect, clean, maintain, and repair your AC. You can book a service appointment at a convenient time, and our team will take care of the rest—saving you the time and hassle of visiting a service center.

Can I choose my preferred time?

Yes, you can choose a preferred date and time for your AC service. Simply select a convenient time slot while booking, and our technician will arrive at your doorstep as scheduled. We aim to provide flexible appointments that fit easily into your daily routine.

Do you service commercial ACs?

Yes, we provide **commercial AC servicing** for offices, shops, restaurants, hotels, and other business spaces. Our experienced technicians can handle routine maintenance, deep cleaning, repairs, cooling issues, and other AC requirements. We offer flexible service options to help keep your commercial AC systems running efficiently and reliably.
